Why we're building this

Three pillars. One community.

Spillin' Tea was founded on food, friends and a love for the arts—three things that too often get overlooked in the day-to-day.

Our job is to create an experience where the community can slow down, share a table and take part in something together. Come laugh with friends, scream for the band, sing along, discover a local artist and play a part in the night.

The art vision

A living Gulf Coast canvas

Inspired by the creative energy of Ocean Springs and the wider Mississippi Gulf Coast, the art will live on the walls, across the shipping containers, on the stage and inside the event calendar.

Built into the architecture

The building becomes the gallery

Graffiti-inspired murals, bold container artwork and rotating installations will make the venue feel collected, creative and unmistakably local—not like art was added after the fact.

Local artists first

Showcase the people making it

Artist features, live painting and rotating work will give Gulf Coast creators a visible platform inside a high-energy community venue.

Sunday brunch

Art auctions with a purpose

Rotating Sunday brunch auctions will promote featured artists, with auction proceeds going directly to the artist whose work is sold.

Art partnerships

Built with the community

We plan to work with local art organizations to curate showcases, connect with artists and keep the program rooted in the Gulf Coast.

Music is art

Every stage tells a story

Full bands, acoustic sessions, DJs and young local musicians make the performing arts part of the same mission—not a separate attraction.
