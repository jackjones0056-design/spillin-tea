Welcome to Spillin' Tea

The whole experience, one table at a time.

Spillin' Tea was founded on three pillars: food, friends and a love for the arts—three things that tend to get overlooked in everyday life. Our job is to create a place where the community can slow down, gather around the table and experience music, art and entertainment together.
